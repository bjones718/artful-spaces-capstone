Artful Spaces

A digital platform connecting local artists with businesses and property owners through curated art staging, rotation, commission services, virtual staging tools, and a browsable artist gallery.

01 / The Problem

Local art needs better visibility.

Artists often struggle to promote and sell their work, while businesses and property owners lack a simple way to discover, preview, rent, rotate, or commission local artwork.

02 / The Solution

A platform for artists, clients, and spaces.

Artful Spaces connects local artists with commercial and residential interiors through curated discovery, virtual staging, artist profiles, and service-based art placement.

03 / The App

Core Platform Features

Artist Gallery

Browse artists by style, medium, color, size, location, and availability.

Virtual Staging

Preview artwork in offices, lobbies, homes, galleries, and commercial interiors.

Rotation + Commission

Request artwork rotations, custom commissions, and curated staging packages.
