Artful Spaces

A digital platform connecting local artists with businesses and property owners through curated art staging, rotation, commission services, virtual staging tools, and a browsable artist gallery.

01 / Problem

Local art needs better visibility.

Artists often struggle to promote and sell their work, while businesses, property owners, and real estate teams lack a simple way to discover, preview, rent, rotate, or commission local artwork.

Opportunity

Interior spaces can become living galleries.

Artful Spaces creates a bridge between artists and commercial interiors by making local art easier to browse, stage, rotate, purchase, and commission.

02 / Methodology

Research, prototyping, critique, and iteration.

Research

Study local artist needs, staging services, gallery models, audience behavior, and comparable digital platforms.

Feedback

Gather input from artists, business owners, property managers, classmates, instructor critique, and external reviewers.

Iteration

Refine the brand system, app structure, visual language, user flow, prototype screens, and final case study presentation.

03 / The App

Core platform features.

Artist Gallery

Browse artists by style, medium, color, size, location, and availability.

Virtual Staging

Preview artwork in offices, lobbies, homes, galleries, and commercial interiors.

Rotation + Commission

Request rotating art placements, custom commissions, and curated staging packages.

04 / Gallery

Brand, prototype, and campaign visuals.

Logo System
App Screens
Virtual Staging
Artist Profiles
Print Extension

05 / Deliverables

Final project components.

Planned deliverables include the Artful Spaces brand system, app prototype, gallery interface, virtual staging concept, campaign assets, process documentation, citation archive, and final presentation.

Capstone Requirements

Online, accessible, and documented.

This webpage will act as the public case study and online gallery for the complete capstone project.

06 / Summary

Artful Spaces reframes interiors as platforms for local creativity.

Through branding, UI/UX, web design, and campaign development, this capstone explores how a digital service can support artists while giving businesses and property owners a more meaningful way to activate their spaces.
