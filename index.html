MA Graphic + Web Design Capstone

A digital platform connecting local artists with businesses and property owners through curated art staging, rotation, commission services, virtual staging tools, and a browsable artist gallery.

Explore the Project →
01

Problem

Local artists need better visibility and access to interior spaces.

Artists often struggle to promote and sell their work, while businesses, property owners, and real estate teams lack a simple way to discover, preview, rent, rotate, or commission local artwork.

Opportunity

Artful Spaces reframes offices, lobbies, homes, and commercial interiors as living galleries for local creativity.
